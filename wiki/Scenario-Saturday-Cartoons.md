# Scenario: Saturday morning cartoons

Your family channel should play cartoons, and only cartoons, on Saturday
mornings, then go back to normal.

## 1. One show for the cartoons

Add each cartoon series to the lineup as its own show, or, if you have
many, combine them into one [wheel](Wheels) so they do not swamp the rest
of the week.

If the cartoons should air only on Saturday mornings, tick **Only air when
a rule picks it** on each of them. If they can also appear during the week,
leave that off.

## 2. The rule

On the **Rules** tab, click **New rule…**:

- **General**: name it "Saturday Morning Cartoons" and choose **Take over
  the channel**.
- **When**: tick **Sat** under Days. Tick **Only during certain hours**,
  starting at 7:00 AM and ending at 12:00 PM.
- **What plays**: tick the cartoons. For each one, you can tick **Change
  how much plays at a time** and choose, say, **~1 hr**, so the morning
  moves between them in blocks.

## What Saturday looks like

Whatever is on at 7:00 finishes, then the cartoons take turns among
themselves until noon. The last cartoon may start a little before noon and
run past it; after that the regular lineup resumes the round it paused.

The Preview tab lists the next ten turns, so by late Friday you can watch
Saturday morning take shape.

## Variations

- **Weekday mornings before school**: tick Mon through Fri and set the
  hours to 6:30 AM to 8:00 AM.
- **Cartoons join the lineup rather than replace it**: choose **Mix into
  the lineup** instead. The cartoons then take turns alongside the regular
  shows during those hours.
