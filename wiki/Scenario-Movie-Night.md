# Scenario: Friday movie night

You want a specific movie to start at exactly 8:00 PM this Friday, with the
channel back to normal afterward. Rules cannot promise an exact time, so
this is a [special](Specials).

## Book it

Open the channel's Properties, go to the **Specials** tab, and click **New
special…**:

- **Starts**: Friday at 8:00 PM.
- **What airs**: **Programs I pick**. Search for the movie under
  **Program 1**. To add a second feature, click **Add a program**.
- **Ends (optional)**: leave it blank. The special ends when the last movie
  ends.

Click **Preview**. It lists the movie with its exact times, shows which
regular program gets pushed until after it, and says how long the channel
will be off the air before 8:00. Click **Save special**.

## What Friday looks like

Regular programming plays whole programs until the last one that can finish
before 8:00. If the next program would overrun, it waits for its show's
next turn instead, and the gap shows as **Off air** with a "Programming
resumes at 8:00 PM" screen. At 8:00 the movie starts. When it ends, the
next show in the rotation takes its turn.

The gap can only be avoided for the program that is on the air when you
save. Book the special during that program with a start inside it, and the
preview offers **Stop it at the special's start**; see [Cutting
in](Specials#cutting-in). A special saved days ahead never cuts a future
program.

## Every Friday

Specials do not repeat. For a regular movie night, book one special per
week, or settle for "Friday evening" with a rule: **Take over the
channel**, **Fri**, 8:00 PM to 11:00 PM, playing a movie collection. The
movie then starts at the first boundary after 8:00 rather than on the dot.
