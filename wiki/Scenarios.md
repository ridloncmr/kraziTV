# Scenarios

Each scenario is a short recipe for a common setup, using the pieces the
rest of the wiki explains. Pick the one closest to what you want.

| I want to...                                             | Read                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Play Christmas specials and movies through December      | [Christmas in December](Scenario-Christmas)             |
| Run cartoons on Saturday mornings                        | [Saturday morning cartoons](Scenario-Saturday-Cartoons) |
| Air grown-up shows only late at night                    | [Late night after 8 PM](Scenario-Late-Night)            |
| Put a movie on at exactly 8:00 PM on Friday              | [Friday movie night](Scenario-Movie-Night)              |
| Hand a channel over to one theme for an evening or a day | [Take over a channel](Scenario-Take-Over)               |
| Understand what happens when two rules apply at once     | [When two rules overlap](Scenario-Rules-Overlap)        |

Horror all October, a Star Wars day on May the 4th, and Thanksgiving
specials are all the Christmas scenario with different dates. The simplest
channels, one series in episode order or a movie collection in release
order, need no scenario: [First run](First-Run) builds one.

New to rules? [Christmas in December](Scenario-Christmas) is the fullest
walk-through.
