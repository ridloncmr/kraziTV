# Scenario: Take over a channel

Sometimes a channel should drop its regular lineup and play one thing for a
while: a whole franchise on a rainy Sunday, a birthday kid's favorite show
all evening, a themed day. There are two tools, and the choice comes down
to one question: does it have to start at an exact time?

| Need                                               | Tool                                           |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| "This evening is all Star Wars, starting whenever" | A rule that takes over the channel             |
| "Every film in order, starting at noon sharp"      | A special playing a collection from the start  |
| "Only this collection until midnight, exact edges" | A special playing a collection until a set end |
| "Start it right now"                               | A special, with **Start now**                  |

## With a rule

Rules are soft: they start at the next program boundary, never cut
anything, and leave no off-air gaps. For a one-off day:

1. Make sure the themed collection is on the lineup as a show.
2. **New rule…**: **Take over the channel**, **Dates** set to **Between two
   dates** with today as both ends, hours if you want them, and the themed
   show under **What plays**.
3. Delete the rule afterward, since rules have no off switch.

The regular lineup pauses and resumes exactly where it left off when the
rule ends.

## With a special

Specials are exact: they start to the second, can cut into what is
airing, and can leave off-air gaps when nothing fits. For a day of
everything in order:

1. **New special…** on the Specials tab.
2. **Starts** at the time you want. **What airs**: **A collection, from the
   start**, with the collection and its play order. Leave **Ends
   (optional)** blank so it ends with the last program.
3. **Preview**, check the list, **Save special**.

To take over immediately, set the start a few minutes ahead but inside the
program airing now, preview, and click **Start now**. Viewers stay tuned
and see the switch.

## Mixing the two

A takeover rule for the day plus a special at a fixed time inside it works
fine. The special wins its slot, and the rule fills the rest.
